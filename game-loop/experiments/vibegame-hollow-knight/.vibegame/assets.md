## Authoritative visual direction

`assets/concepts/concept.png` is the style reference and is awaiting human confirmation.

- Moth Knight: ivory crescent mask, midnight teal mantle, slim crescent blade.
- Briar Warden: branching bone crown, black-to-rust cloak, thorn lance.
- Arena: empty moonlit blue-black ruined sanctuary and pale combat floor.
- VFX: cyan/white player power; ember/rust and black ash boss danger.

Krea2 is text-to-image only. Sheets are therefore anchored by this explicit character bible, keyed to transparent backgrounds, and registered as deterministic atlas cells.
